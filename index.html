The problem

Talent is scattered across campus. Nobody can see the whole picture.

One student can train a neural network but has never touched Figma. Another can design a beautiful interface but has no one to wire it up. They sit three buildings apart and never meet.

Skills live in scattered resumes, group chats, and half-finished GitHub repos. There's no shared map — so teams form by who you already know, not by who actually complements you.

MindMesh turns that scattered talent into a searchable, matchable graph — so the right people find each other before the deadline, not after.

How it works

From scattered skills to a matched team

Four stages, one continuous loop. The mesh gets smarter every time a student updates their profile or finishes a project.

01

Build a real profile

List your skills, past projects, achievements, and what you actually want to learn next — not just a resume, a living skill graph.

02

The AI reads the gaps

MindMesh analyzes your profile against your goals and flags what's missing — a language, a framework, a design skill — before you ask.

03

Get matched, not searched

The engine pairs complementary students — a Python/AI builder with a React/UI-UX designer — into teams that actually cover each other's blind spots.

04

Track growth, find openings

A dashboard shows your progress over time and surfaces hackathons, internships, and projects sized to where you are right now.
